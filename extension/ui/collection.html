What this reads, and where it goes

Stashling reads the pages you ask it to, in the tab you already have open, and writes what it finds to this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account and no server. Export writes a plain JSONL file to your disk; import reads one back.

Sites it can currently reach: —. Access is asked for one site at a time, the first time you collect from it, and you can revoke it in your browser's extension settings at any point.
